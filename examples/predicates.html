Predicates

The ten named predicates and the DE-9IM matrix they are read from, on a plain <canvas>. Each operand can be a polygon, a line or a point. Drag either one; positions snap to a 10 pixel grid, so edges can line up exactly and the boundary cases — touches, a point on an edge — are reachable by hand. Every answer is exact, so a shape that looks touching but is off by a pixel says so.
