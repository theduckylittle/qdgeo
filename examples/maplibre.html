MapLibre GL JS

MapLibre GL JS renders vector maps in WebGL. qdgeo is planar and reads no coordinate system, so this page does its geometry in Web Mercator metres — a buffer distance is metres — and converts back to longitude and latitude for the map. toArrays() builds the nested form a result needs here. Drag either shape by its centre handle.
