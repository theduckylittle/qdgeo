MapLibre GL JS

MapLibre wants GeoJSON, so this page converts on the way out — a few lines, and on results this small it costs well under a millisecond. The geometry is computed in Web Mercator metres and converted back to longitude and latitude for the map. Drag either shape by its centre handle.
