deck.gl

The result crosses into deck.gl as binary. qdgeo returns one coordinate array plus ring and polygon ends; deck.gl's SolidPolygonLayer wants flat positions plus startIndices and vertexValid. Going from one to the other loops over rings and polygons, never over coordinates, so no coordinate is read or written in JavaScript on the way out.

See src/deck-binary.js for the conversion, which is 30 lines.
